# Project Overview :

This project involves data analysis and predictive modeling for an ecommerce company based in New York City that sells clothing online and also offers in-store personal stylist sessions. The goal of the analysis is to help the company determine whether to focus more on improving their mobile app experience or their website platform, based on how these platforms influence customer spending behavior.
We use Linear Regression to model the relationship between customer characteristics, usage behavior, and their annual spending.

##################       Objectives         ####################

1. Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on customer data.

2. Understand customer behavior across different platforms.

3. Build a Linear Regression model to predict annual customer spending.

4. Identify whether the mobile app or website contributes more to revenue.

5. Provide data-driven business recommendations.

##################           Tech Stack       #######################

1. Python 3.0
2. Pandas
3. NumPy
4. Matplotlib and Seaborn
5. Scikit-Learn
6. Google Colab Notebook

####################        Dataset Information    ####################

1. Email: Customer email address

2. Address: Customer address

3. Avatar: Avatar image filename

4. Avg. Session Length: Average session time on the website

5. Time on App: Average time spent on the mobile app

6. Time on Website: Average time spent on the website

7. Length of Membership: How long the customer has been a member

8. Yearly Amount Spent: Annual amount spent on ecommerce purchases (target variable)

####################      Results Summary       #######################

Takeaway 1 : The mobile app usage was a stronger predictor of yearly spending compared to website usage.
Takeaway 2 :  The length of membership also had a significant positive correlation with spending.

####################      Recommendation         ######################

The company should consider investing more in enhancing the mobile app experience while maintaining a consistent website presence.
Optimizing the app can lead to higher customer engagement and revenue growth.
